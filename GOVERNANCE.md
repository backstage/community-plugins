# Governance

The Backstage Community Plugins project area is governed according to the model specified in the [GOVERNANCE.md](https://github.com/backstage/community/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md) file located in the `backstage/community` repository. We encourage all contributors and maintainers to review these guidelines to understand their roles and responsibilities.

## Community Plugins Maintainers

Community Plugins Maintainers are the [Project Area Maintainers](https://github.com/backstage/community/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md#project-area-maintainer) for this repository, listed under [Community Plugins](https://github.com/backstage/backstage/blob/master/OWNERS.md#community-plugins) in the Backstage `OWNERS.md`. The current Community Plugins Maintainers are the members of the [@backstage/community-plugins-maintainers](https://github.com/orgs/backstage/teams/community-plugins-maintainers) team.

## Plugin Owners

Plugin Owners are [Plugin Maintainers](https://github.com/backstage/community/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md#plugin-maintainer) as defined in the Backstage governance model. They are the people listed in the [CODEOWNERS](./.github/CODEOWNERS) file for one or more workspaces, and are members of the [@backstage/community-plugin-owners](https://github.com/orgs/backstage/teams/community-plugin-owners) team.
